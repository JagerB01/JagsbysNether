Nether Agriculture v0.1.5 — First Bedrock Test Build

1. Import Nether_Agriculture_v0.1.5.mcaddon into Bedrock 1.21.100 or newer.
   Activate both the Behavior and Resource packs on a disposable world.
   Enable cheats for the starter-kit command. Beta APIs are not required.
   If import or scripts fail, capture the Content Log errors.
   Replace the previous packs with v0.1.5; activate both updated packs.

2. Enter the Nether and run:
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:kit

3. Make a small soul-soil bed and safely contain lava beside it at soil height.
   Hoe the top of the soul soil. Farmland within four horizontal blocks of lava
   should START DRY and take about fourteen seconds to show the heated texture. Lava below the soil does
   not count. Soil without lava should remain farmland permanently.
   Place and walk on farmland: both should use soul-soil sounds. Tilling should
   play the normal hoe-tilling sound. Vanilla-hoe arm motion is still unresolved.

4. Plant Sorgum Seeds, Polyps and Grollet Seeds on the farmland; plant Scarberries
   on soul soil, netherrack or nylium. Check sprites, selection boxes and names.
   Plants cannot be placed outside the Nether with their planting items.

5. Try ordinary bone meal (no growth) and Wither Bone Meal (crop advancement).
   Switch to survival to check fertilizer consumption and hoe durability.
   Successful Wither Bone Meal use should play a sound and show growth particles.
   Its native interaction handler needs a fresh arm-motion check in both camera
   views. Failed growth should not consume fertilizer or play effects.
   Mature Sorgum should drop one Sorgum and zero to three seeds; immature Sorgum one seed.
   Retest whether nearby crop growth still stutters.
   Break mature Sorgum and Polyps and verify the harvested items. Ripe Scarberry
   bushes should yield berries when interacted with, then regrow from stage one.

6. Leave a clear space next to a mature gourd stem with Nether ground below it.
   Wait for fruit, break it, and confirm the stem can fruit again. It must not
   replace an existing block. Shear a gourd to get a carved gourd and four seeds.

7. All recipes should appear with the crafting book's craftable-only filter off.
   Test recipes and food in survival. Three Sorgum craft Sorgum Bread; three Blistered
   Sorgum craft Blistered Sorgum Bread. Scarberries + glass bottle craft a Scarberry
   Bottle. A Polyp should cook into a Baked Polyp. A gourd + redstone + egg make
   Grollet Pie. Check fire resistance from Blistered Sorgum Bread and glass bottle
   return/Wither removal from Scarberry Bottle.

8. Optional: inspect the rare Blistered Sorgum crop without waiting for mutation:
   /setblock ~ ~ ~ jagsby_nether:blistered_rye_crop
   Run this above Soul Soil Farmland in the Nether, then break the crop.

9. Reload the world and confirm pack activation and scripts still work. If
   testing with Nether Settlements, activate its pack too; this first crop port
   does not replace settlement farms or automatically populate them with crops.

Natural crop patches, bush contact damage, farmland trampling/scorching, custom
tools, custom brewing and End content are not included in this first test build.

10. Check names in creative inventory, item tooltips, crafting results and bales.
    Expect Sorgum, Sorgum Seeds/Bread/Bale and Blistered Sorgum variants;
    Grollet, Grollet Seeds/Pie/Stem, Carved Grollet and Grollet Lantern.
    In an upgraded test world, old items and planted crops should still exist
    and work with the updated names. Save/reload and verify them again.

11. On a newly tilled plot near lava, immediately try Wither Bone Meal. It
    should grow the crop and consume one item in survival, without instantly
    heating the soil. Repeat on dry farmland with no lava. All plots should grow
    naturally; fully
    heated soil should grow faster on average, not block growth on dry soil.
    Using fertilizer on a mature crop should not consume it or play effects.
    Remove lava and check gradual cooling; the soil must remain tilled.
    Previously heated farmland in an upgraded world should retain its heat.

12. Inspect Sorgum, Polyps, Scarberries and Grollet stems from several angles
    while they grow. Check for flickering or double images, including after
    fertilizer use. Compare arm/held-item movement in first-person and third-person
    while tilling and fertilizing; successful planting remains a useful comparison.

13. Compare dry and lava-heated beds planted with Sorgum, Polyps and Grollet.
    Dry growth is three times slower on average, so use several crops rather
    than judging a single plant. /gamerule randomTickSpeed 100 can speed this up
    in a test world; restore the Bedrock default of 1 afterward. Confirm Grollet
    can produce fruit without lava. Harvest everything and wait: dry soil must
    remain tilled even without plants. Save/reload and recheck these plots.

14. The previous scripted arm-animation attempts did not work on the reported
    26.52 client and have been removed. Fertilizer now uses the native crop
    interaction callback; check its motion in first-person and third-person,
    survival and creative, sneaking and holding use. Tilling with vanilla hoes
    still has no confirmed arm-motion fix. Check normal mining and attacking too.
