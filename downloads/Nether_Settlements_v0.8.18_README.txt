Nether Settlements v0.8.18 - Distant Village Search

Replace the previous Nether Settlements pack.
Natural village search uses grid points 96-128 horizontal blocks from each Nether player, searching across neighboring 192-block regions. Each point can still compare local centers within 12 blocks, and selected centers must be at least 96 blocks from all Nether players.
Natural placement checks the entire footprint of every selected building, road, and center against a 32-block horizontal clearance around every Nether player. The check runs before commitment and during placement. A player approaching can interrupt placement; already placed pieces are not rolled back, and the center remains last.
Search points with unloaded terrain are skipped without force-loading chunks or consuming their cooldown. Partially loaded/blocked layouts can still fail and retry later. Low simulation/loading distances can limit available distant sites; generation does not fall back to placing next to players.
Minimum distance between recorded village centers is reduced from 384 to 256 blocks. This is minimum separation, not a promised interval. Standalone one-house sites still have no village exclusion radius.
Completed region markers correspond to the region of the searched site, rather than the player's region.
Removed all 24 obsolete preassembled village structures and their 24 feature definitions; any references in weighted feature lists are pruned. Individual terrain-aware building templates remain intact.
The 52 additional building templates, 0/1-house placement rules, roads, camps, and outposts are retained. Existing villages are not moved or removed. Existing explored terrain remains eligible under this scripted system.
Manual test commands continue to operate at the player's requested location, bypassing automatic player-distance restrictions:
/scriptevent nether_settlements:test crimson
/scriptevent nether_settlements:test warped
Finder:
/scriptevent nether_settlements:find village
/scriptevent nether_settlements:find nearest
Potential finder coordinates remain exploration guides, not confirmed villages. To trigger distant searches, explore the surrounding area rather than waiting directly on the candidate coordinate.

Validation: 41 automated geometry and mocked Bedrock runtime tests passed, including multi-region distant candidates, clearance for buildings and block writes, cancellation on player approach, unloaded-point handling, 256-block spacing boundary, and retained village/building behavior. All feature/structure references validated after removal. Live Minecraft gameplay verification remains outstanding.
