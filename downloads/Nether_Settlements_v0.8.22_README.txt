Nether Settlements v0.8.22 - Village Spacing

Replace v0.8.21 with this version.
Minimum center-to-center village spacing reduced from 256 to 200 horizontal blocks. This is a minimum separation, not a guaranteed spawn interval.
Removes all four command-test prototypes: crimson_cottage, warped_porch_house, open_forge, and basalt_lodge. They were never in natural village pools, so natural building variety is unchanged.
If the standalone Nether Building Prototypes behavior pack is still enabled on the world, disable it separately; this update removes prototypes from Nether Settlements only. Already placed structures are not deleted.

Natural roadside templates per style (crimson and warped):
11 homes: 8 small houses, 2 medium houses, 1 big house.
11 profession buildings: armorer, 2 butcher shops, cartographer, fisher cottage, fletcher, mason, shepherd, tannery, tool smith, weaponsmith.
2 libraries and 2 temples.
2 Nether wart farms (small and large).
3 animal pens and 2 stables.
2 meeting areas and 1 accessory plot.
Total: 36 templates per style, 72 across both. 26 enclosed buildings and 10 outdoor amenities per style.
Farms and outdoor amenities do not count toward the two-building village-center requirement. A one-building site still places only that building and does not contribute to village spacing.
Village centers, roads, naturally generated camps/tents, and outposts retain their existing templates and settings. Terrain sampling, eligibility caching, and corrected fence-gate states are retained.
