Nether Building Prototypes v0.1.0

Separate behavior pack; enable alongside Nether Settlements. No natural generation rules or scripts are included.
Four standalone prototype designs. These are review templates, not yet wired into village pools or equipped with jigsaw connectors.
Stand on a flat clear test area, with at least 15 blocks of space above and 14 blocks in the positive Z direction. Cheats required. Templates face negative Z.
Each command places a building five blocks in positive Z, with its floor one block below your feet. Interior air clears blocks inside the template footprint. Move to a different clear area before placing another design.

/structure load nether_prototypes/crimson_cottage ~ ~-1 ~5
/structure load nether_prototypes/warped_porch_house ~ ~-1 ~5
/structure load nether_prototypes/open_forge ~ ~-1 ~5
/structure load nether_prototypes/basalt_lodge ~ ~-1 ~5

Crimson cottage: 9 x 10 x 11 (width x height x depth), dark masonry and crimson timber with a gabled roof.
Warped porch house: 11 x 12 x 14, smooth basalt walls, warped timber and a covered front porch.
Open forge: 13 x 12 x 12, wide open workshop, front awning, enclosed magma hearth and masonry chimney. No flowing lava or active fire.
Basalt lodge: 11 x 15 x 14, two floors, interior stairs, front balcony and warped roof.

Validated little-endian NBT roundtrip, palette identifiers against Bedrock 1.21.100 block IDs, index bounds, doorway pairs, interior access, lighting, and absence of template markers. Live Minecraft placement and appearance remain unverified.
