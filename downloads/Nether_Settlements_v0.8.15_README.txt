Nether Settlements v0.8.15 - Open Terrain Search

Replace the previous behavior pack with this version.
Natural villages now survey ground around Nether players and nearby grid points within 64 blocks, comparing local terrain within 12 blocks of each surveyed point.
The search runs every five seconds when no village is being assembled; assembly yields across game ticks.
Failed sites have temporary ten-minute cooldowns. Other nearby cells remain eligible immediately. No permanent failure flags are written.
Regions are marked complete only after a village finishes with at least one house. Old terrain-attempt flags are ignored.
Recorded successful village centers must be at least 384 blocks apart. This is a minimum separation, not a guaranteed spawn interval.
Center-only remnants are recorded and avoided within 96 blocks to prevent repeated overlapping placements.
Automatic placement operates below the roof on supported natural floors from Y33 upwards. Existing terrain checks still protect structures from lava, cliffs, and ceilings.
Villages can generate in already explored terrain. Camps and outposts still require newly generated chunks; their rarity is unchanged.

Finder in the Nether with cheats enabled:
/scriptevent nether_settlements:find village
/scriptevent nether_settlements:find nearest
Potential coordinates are exploration guides, not confirmed villages.
Older villages that were never indexed cannot be located by this finder.

Validation: 30 automated geometry and mocked Bedrock runtime tests passed, including natural generation on netherrack, soul sand, and soul soil; failed-site recovery; spacing; and legacy attempt-flag recovery. No live Minecraft verification was available.
