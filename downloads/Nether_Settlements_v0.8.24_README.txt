Nether Settlements v0.8.24 - Center Palette

Replace v0.8.23 with this pack. Applies to newly generated settlements only;
existing village floors are not automatically changed.

Completes the netherrack-to-basalt replacement in meeting areas stored under
houses/meeting_point_*, which the previous town_centers-only update missed.
Original, sanitized terrain, Crimson, Warped, and retained ruined templates
are updated. Native jigsaw floor final states and catalog floor metadata match.

The surface material selector now assigns 20% smooth basalt and 80% basalt,
up from 10% smooth basalt. Roads and center/meeting-area floors use the same
coordinate hash; small individual pieces can vary from the overall percentage.
Other materials, geometry, doors, connectors, layout RNG and village spacing
are unchanged. Invisible names for piglin defenders are retained.

Validated 46 center/meeting templates (46 changed): removed 629 missed netherrack cells; {'minecraft:basalt': 1773, 'minecraft:smooth_basalt': 456}; 46 native floor marker final states.
Additional checks: all structure geometry/non-palette blocks preserved,
NBT read/write round trips, catalog/pool asset resolution, JavaScript syntax,
road distribution and material-hash agreement. A 262,144-coordinate road
sample measured 19.87% smooth basalt; 128 catalog assets and 8 pools resolve.
No live Bedrock run available.

Generate NEW villages to test both themes:
/scriptevent nether_settlements:test crimson
/scriptevent nether_settlements:test warped
Inspect fountains and all meeting-area variants, plus connecting roads.
Expect no netherrack in their authored floors, and more smooth basalt.
