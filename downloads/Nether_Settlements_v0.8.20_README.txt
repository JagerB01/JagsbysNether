Nether Settlements v0.8.20 - Eligible Terrain Surveys

Replace the previous Nether Settlements pack.
Before surveying ground, headroom, or slope, reject site samples inside the 256-block radius of a recorded village center, inside a completed region, under a retry cooldown, or directly over a player.
Completed-region eligibility is cached once per region per survey. Recorded village locations are read once at the start of the survey, as in v0.8.19.
The accessible-area traversal uses Dimension.isChunkLoaded when available. Ineligible chunks then require no block reads; the script performs only chunk-connectivity checks. Engines without that method use one block read per visited chunk as an accessibility probe, never a floor/headroom scan for an excluded candidate.
Traversing excluded chunks remains necessary to discover eligible loaded ground beyond them. Skipping connectivity entirely would wrongly stop searches when a player stands near an existing village.
The 256-block rule applies to proposed centers. A valid center just outside the boundary can still survey supporting terrain or building footprints extending inside it. Those limited boundary samples are intentional.
Terrain quality remains the ranking criterion, with no spawn-distance band or preference for farther sites. Layout rules, 52 extra templates, standalone houses, camp/outpost rarity, and removal of obsolete preassembled villages are retained.
