Jagsby's Underworld v0.2.114 — combined personal test build.

Village subsystem: Jagsby's Nether Settlements v0.8.24, including its original
structures, source notices and license. The complete original release notes
and LICENSE.txt are retained under behavior_pack/docs/settlements/.

Agriculture subsystem: Jagsby's Nether Agriculture v0.1.6, adapted from
SuitedLlama's Agriculture Progression 1.0.1 for Java 1.16.4. Original crop/item
textures and license notices are retained. Original credits and license text
are under behavior_pack/docs/agriculture/ and in the resource pack.

Cover artwork: supplied by JagerB01 for the combined pack-selection icons.
Standalone releases remain separately available in the repository.

Lava Fishing v1.3.1: TeamSunset/Sunset (teddyxlandlee, Wei, Karth, Kun,
chen114514). Original rod/fish item textures from the user-supplied
JAR. Fishing adaptation and these assets use AGPLv3; the full license and
source metadata are bundled in docs/lava_fishing/. Existing subsystem licenses
remain unchanged. Upstream: https://github.com/TeamSunset/lavafishing

Vanilla bobber geometry/texture and sounds resolve from Minecraft's built-in resources.
Behavior reference: user-supplied AdvancedFishing by Renderphoenix and devsuki;
native-physics casting, surface anchoring and particle-line techniques were
reviewed. No AdvancedFishing assets, source code, fish or gear are bundled.

Obsidian rod icon: user-supplied ObsidianFishingRod.png, copied unchanged from ObsidianFishingRod.zip.

Skitterchest models, textures and animations adapted from the user-supplied NetherPlus Add-on MA v3 by BubbleStudio and MarcoGamer. Only the three chest pets and their visual dependencies are included.

Skitterchest opening/closing audio: LargeMeat.wav and RawLargeMeat.wav from the user-supplied Eating Animations And Sounds SE V1.9.4 archive (Nexus mod 42602). See docs/skitterchests/SOUND_SOURCE_METADATA.json for original filenames and hashes.

Native chest-minecart client definition adapted from Mojang/bedrock-samples, with a helper-specific skin marker controlling render scale; normal chest minecarts retain scale 1.

MineScape v1.5.18 compatibility: relic item IDs, normal random-spawner pool and
level-1 initialization lore derived from the user-supplied MineScape archive.
No MineScape textures, item definitions or gameplay scripts are bundled.

Hongos: normal Crimson/Warped mushroom models, textures, animations and egg
artwork adapted from the user-supplied NetherPlus Add-on MA v3 by BubbleStudio
and MarcoGamer. HONGOS_SOURCE.json records original asset paths and hashes.

Infernal Lords / essential support / enchanted-mob systems: Alylica's Dungeons
