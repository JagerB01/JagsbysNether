Jagsby's Underworld v1.0.4 — release and regression checklist

Automated validation: 526 checks. These cover assets, reference integrity, actual
BP-event pipeline models, scripted state and scheduling. They do not execute a
Bedrock client, native trial encounters, pathfinding, rendering or Realm profiling.
Use OPERATIONS.md for commands. Retain tests and build tools in the repository.

1. Realm upgrade and idle operation
   Import Jagsbys_Underworld_v1.0.4.mcaddon; activate its BP and RP together.
   Verify existing builds, player items, Skitterchest inventories and MineScape
   progression persist. Confirm a saved build resumes after load. Startup test-kit
   messages should be absent. lords_status should report tracing:false by default.
   First 1.0 load clears only a stale next_giant choice, once; later choices work.
   Enable the MineScape Food Buffs compatibility BP and original RP for food buffs;
   keep C&C v1.1.0 enabled if used. Do not duplicate supplied standalone packs.

2. Generation and recovery
   Villages require four houses plus one small farm and 256-block center spacing.
   Confirm farms plant/replant matching Nether crops on soul soil, with composters,
   and farmers harvest them. Villagers should collect Sorgum/Polyps as breeding food.
   Small Arenas are Basalt Deltas only. All three giants retain equal selection
   after the full eligibility comparison; foundations follow authored footprints.
   Verify module expansion, loot, dispenser traps and player-proximity pauses.
   structures_status must distinguish loading, elapsed stage and retry timing.
   Check chunk-load fallback, cleanup and resumed placement on the actual Realm.
   Confirm structure spacing/protected blocks and mined rejected village sites.

3. Trials and combat
   Confirm normal and ominous trials spawn, count deaths/transformations, complete,
   reward players and enter cooldown. Caster family weights stay 25% Wraith, 25%
   Necromancer, 50% Brute. Each trial caster rolls 33% enchanted independently;
   use a substantial sample, not a few mobs. Brutes stay ordinary.
   Check existing recorded spawner updates and native Nether break/explosion
   protection. Confirm optional Mega Fortress modules retain ordinary spawners.
   Fight all three bosses on Hard, both awakened and directly summoned. Verify
   teleports, projectiles, invulnerability, shields, summons and complete phases.
   Nerezhal's two Frozen Zombies and Baelgor's Blazes should always be enchanted,
   with real stats/glint/perks; verify minion caps and cleanup.
   Check Wraith fire/poison, Necromancer shots/summons and enchanted variants.
   Enable lords_trace explicitly for counters and disable it afterward. Enable
   boss_damage_watch only for health measurements; confirm it stops on off/leave.
   Test unenchanted Netherite, full Protection IV, Resistance and Fire Resistance,
   including simultaneous projectiles. INFERNAL_LORDS_DAMAGE.md records targets;
   actual native health loss is not established by offline damage models.

4. Core gameplay and compatibility
   Test crop tilling/growth/heating, Wither Bone Meal and recipe outputs.
   Fish in each Nether biome and outside the Nether. Check rod cast/reel motion,
   line, sounds, seven fish, junk/treasure, wear and 1.5x fishing XP. Raw fish must
   restore food without old scripted buffs. Missed bites require reeling/recasting.
   Tame Crimson/Warped Skitterchests, open native storage while standing, toggle
   sit/stand while crouched, and check movement/step-up/lava avoidance and loot.
   Matching tamed Hongos deposit only into sitting tamed chests: Warped gold every
   25–35 seconds, Crimson iron every 40–60 seconds while loaded.
   Test Glowsquito hive entry/exit, wax accumulation, shearing and gunpowder crafting.
   Check soul-light spawn suppression at torch 12, lantern 16 and lit campfire 20.
   Verify crafted foods stack to 16 and permit one 20-minute skill buff at a time;
   compare MineScape base/effective primary bonuses with its food_buff_status.

5. Performance and future changes
   Capture idle, exploration, active-generation, fishing and multiplayer profiles.
   Record script workload separately from FPS/native loading and rendering cost.
   Diagnostic tools must be off in the normal-play baseline. A version bump and
   automated checks alone do not prove native Realm performance or completion.

Nylium and trapdoors (v1.0.4)
Use Wither Bone Meal on exposed netherrack in both forest biomes; verify a patch, one-item survival consumption, and no consumption in other biomes. Check new village trapdoors against their supports. Existing builds are not retroactively edited.

Mooring I: apply at anvil with rod main hand/book offhand; verify survival consumes one book, creative retains it, and durability/native enchantments persist. Test melee, arrows and explosions while held, switching slots, armor changes, logout/rejoin, death, Mercury Seed and Combat Poise. mooring_status must report native=1 while held. Confirm damage remains normal and previous resistance returns on unequip. Native attribute access is not verified by the automated mock.

Glowsquito carrying appearance: pollinate a fungus, confirm the orange shroomlight appearance while returning, then verify the normal appearance after depositing and exiting the Glowhusk.
