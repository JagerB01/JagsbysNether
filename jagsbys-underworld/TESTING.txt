Jagsby's Underworld v0.2.109 — current Bedrock checklist

Status: the v0.2.109 gameplay build passed 431 automated checks. These cover
asset/reference integrity and mocked script behavior, not Bedrock client
rendering, native trial activation/rewards, natural encounter frequency or FPS.
There is no Bedrock client in the development workspace. Record actual results
and Content Log messages rather than treating every item below as unverified
or already proven by automation.

1. Installation and upgrades
   Import Jagsbys_Underworld_v0.2.109.mcaddon and activate both Underworld packs.
   Use Bedrock 26.52 with cheats for commands; Beta APIs are not required.
   Disable the separate Nether Settlements/Agriculture packs. Test upgrades on
   a world copy. Normal Underworld updates retain their UUIDs/world records;
   indexes owned by the older standalone pack UUIDs do not automatically transfer.
   Enable MineScape's patched compatibility BP and original RP for food bonuses,
   with only one MineScape BP active. Test C&C v1.1.0 with its BP and RP together.

2. Agriculture
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:kit
   Till soul soil, plant each crop, and use Wither Bone Meal. Check item use,
   sounds, particles and growth. Planted cold soil remains planted; empty fully
   cooled farmland reverts after about 30 loaded seconds. Lava heating takes
   about 14 seconds to reach full heat and improves growth. Verify Grollet fruit
   and the existing crop/food recipes without duplicated actions or effects.

3. Villages
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:test crimson
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:test warped
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:find village
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:status
   Use separate clear test areas. A village must fit at least four residential
   houses and one small farm before any blocks are placed. Large farms are gone.
   Natural targets are 6–8, 9–11 or 12–14 houses, subject to terrain; natural
   village centers must be at least 256 blocks apart. Check the first farm,
   composter, soul-soil/farmland beds, soul torches, roads and native residents.
   Crimson Forest/Nether Wastes use Crimson style and Sorgum; Warped Forest/
   Soul Sand Valley use Warped style and Polyps. Planting uses the actual biome,
   including forced style and biome boundaries. Standalone structures remain
   wart-free without village crop planting; Nether-wart building blocks remain.

4. Villager food and farming
   Give each adult parent 12 raw Sorgum/Polyps (24 total for two unfed parents),
   with a spare valid bed and mobGriefing enabled. Test partial/mixed servings
   and retained stack remainders. Farmers next to their fields/composters should
   harvest and immediately replant nearby ripe custom crops during working hours.
   Walls, non-farmers, babies and mobGriefing=false should prevent custom harvest.
   Do not expect added pathfinding to distant fields or a village return boundary.
   Check C&C appearance, professions, trades and breeding remain intact.

5. Natural landmarks and foundations
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:structures_status
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:structures_diagnostics
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:structures_find
   Explore loaded/open Nether areas and observe complete survey results. Small
   Arenas require Basalt Deltas. For giants, Dragon's largest starting footprint
   is checked first; Large and the Mega keep must also qualify before the equal
   three-way choice. Starting footprints must be completely over lava with clear
   building volume, with bounded optional-module terrain carving. Mega needs only its keep
   initially and grows through connected modules. Check all four required lower chambers (preflighted before the keep),
   bounded optional carving, branch stops, pause/resume and reload recovery.
   Dragon foundations must follow the irregular authored ground base, with no
   square corner fill. Mega bridge spans stay open under decks; only pier feet
   and chamber bases extend to the first existing solid block. Old completed
   foundations/layouts are not rebuilt. Older diagnostic entries can describe
   a previous policy; capture the current version and latest messages.

6. Manual templates
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:template list
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:template dragon_arena 100 32 100
   Use a disposable clear area; these commands can overwrite existing blocks.
   Manual placement bypasses natural suitability and does not add natural
   foundations. Manual Mega places the fixed full assembly, not modular growth.
   See ARENAS.md for dimensions and other template names.

7. Trial encounters and updates — requires native live verification
   Test Small/Large/Dragon arenas and the central Mega keep. Every arena/keep
   spawner is a trial spawner: Piglin Brutes by default, with Strays assigned when
   two earlier Brute spawners are within 16 blocks in 3D. Other optional Mega module
   spawners remain ordinary. Check activation, actual mobs, wave completion,
   cooldowns, player scaling and ominous behavior in Survival.
   Test player breaking (including Creative) and explosions in the Nether.
   Operator commands are still an override; Overworld trial spawners and ordinary
   module spawners are not covered by this protection.
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:encounters_status
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:encounters_update
   On an encounter-data upgrade, recorded locations update once without rebuilding
   their structure. Confirm removed spawners/player blocks remain untouched and
   unchanged data does no migration work on later world loads. Changed data resets
   affected native encounter/cooldown state. Old manual placements need their
   original origin registered, for example:
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:encounters_register dragon_arena 100 32 100
   Vault reward/key configuration and end-game loot progression remain unfinished;
   native vault blocks alone do not prove those systems function.

8. Fishing
   /scriptevent jagsby_nether:fishing_kit
   Follow FISHING.md: rod recipe, casting/line art, sounds, early reels, missed
   bites requiring recast, launched catches, inventory-full pickup, 1.5x vanilla
   XP and fishing enchantments. Check all five specialist biomes, common-only
   catches outside the Nether, dimension changes and reloads. Raw fish restore
   hunger/saturation only. Verify junk/treasure, Luck of the Sea category odds, native enchantments
   and randomized equipment damage. There are no naturally spawning fish or fish spawn eggs.

9. Skitterchests and Hongos
   Check spawn eggs, native movement, one-block steps and lava avoidance. Only
   Crimson/Warped Skitterchests tame; no riding/breeding. Stand to open native
   27-slot storage; crouch to sit/stand. Check blank window heading, no overhead
   name, sound mapping, unpushable seated pets, and following starting at 4 blocks.
   Contents must persist across reloads and drop once on death. Check variant
   death loot/XP and the Blackstone 75% MineScape relic roll over enough trials.
   Matching tamed Hongos within 8 blocks deposit only into sitting tamed chests,
   with any owners: Warped gold every 25–35 seconds, Crimson iron every 40–60.
   Full/missing storage must not create duplicate or dropped nuggets. Unloaded
   time must not accumulate a production backlog. Hongos roam rather than follow
   owners; leads can bring them to an enclosure.

10. Crafted food buffs and integration
    Import the MineScape food compatibility BP before evaluating percentages.
    Check all five recipes, gold names and stacks of 16. A consumed buff lasts
    20 real minutes (offline time counts); another food replaces it and the same
    food refreshes it. Only selected earned primary bonuses double; secondary
    perks, skill tiers, XP and Constitution do not. Use:
    /scriptevent jagsby_nether:food_buff_status
    /scriptevent passiveskills:food_buff_status
    Compare earned percentages before/after, replacement, level changes, expiry
    and reconnect. Custom lava-fishing/crop integration is a separate audit;
    the patch modifies existing MineScape calculations, not every custom activity.

11. Loot, soul lights and performance
    Open fresh generated chests/barrels; test Nether supplies, rare planting
    items and no Nether Wart. Existing placed containers are not refilled.
    Test new hostile spawns inside/outside soul torch 12, lantern 16 and lit
    campfire 20-block ranges. Suppression removes mobs after spawn without XP
    or loot; existing mobs/passive/tamed creatures remain. Arena replacement
    lights must not trigger suppression. Check extinguished campfires separately.
    Capture fresh stationary and exploration CPU profiles, plus generation and
    multiplayer if possible. Compare script work separately from game FPS and
    native terrain/lighting costs. PERFORMANCE.md preserves earlier measurements;
    those are not current-build frame-rate claims.
