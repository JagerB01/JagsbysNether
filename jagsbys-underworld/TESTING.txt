Jagsby's Underworld v0.2.8 — combined test build

1. Import Jagsbys_Underworld_v0.2.8.mcaddon. Confirm both pack-selection entries
   show the supplied Jagsby's Underworld artwork and the v0.2.8 name/version.
   Activate both Behavior and Resources. Use a new test world on Bedrock 26.52,
   with cheats enabled. Disable the separate Nether Settlements and Agriculture
   packs to prevent duplicate script execution. Beta APIs are not required.

2. Enter the Nether. Run /scriptevent jagsby_nether:kit and verify it gives one
   starter kit. Till soul soil with a vanilla hoe, plant each crop and use Wither
   Bone Meal. Check arm motion, sounds, particles, textures and item consumption.

3. Compare planted dry soil, empty dry soil and empty lava-protected soil.
   Planted soil stays tilled and grows crops without lava. Empty fully cold soil
   reverts after about 30 loaded seconds. Lava-protected empty soil stays tilled.
   Lava takes about 14 seconds to fully heat a new plot; growth is faster when
   fully heated. Confirm crops do not flicker and Grollet can produce fruit.

4. Generate a new Crimson village in a clear test area:
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:test crimson
   Check center/meeting floors and roads use basalt with more smooth basalt,
   structures connect correctly, and each residential house has one villager and no piglin defenders.
   Move to another clear area and repeat with:
   /scriptevent nether_settlements:test warped
   Use /scriptevent nether_settlements:find village to check the new village index.

5. Plant and harvest the new crops close to a village. Both subsystems should
   keep working without doubled mobs, kits, sounds, item use or generation jobs.
   Former wart beds should now contain Sorgum or Polyps on soul soil farmland.

6. Check Sorgum Bread, Grollet Pie and Grol-o’-Lantern names/recipes. Test food
   effects in survival. Save/reload and confirm crops, villagers, village lookup
   and both packs still work. Check the Content Log for script or asset errors.

7. Existing-world upgrade testing is optional and should use a copy. Original
   crop/item identifiers are retained, but village records from the old behavior
   pack UUID do not transfer automatically to this new combined pack. A fresh
   world avoids that unverified migration behavior during v0.2.8 testing.

8. Repeat generation with Conquest & Commerce behavior and resource packs
   enabled, then disabled. Check native villagers without it and its matching
   Nether-biome variants with it. Centers and amenities should add no residents.

9. Generate villages in crimson forest and Nether wastes (Sorgum), and warped
   forest and soul sand valley (Polyps). Verify former wart positions use soul
   soil farmland, grow and can be harvested. Force the opposite village style
   to confirm actual biome still selects the crop. Check rotated farms and
   biome boundaries. In basalt deltas, standalone houses and abandoned camps,
   former wart spots should be empty. Keep nether-wart building blocks intact.
   No visible biome probes should appear or remain; check the Content Log.

10. Follow FISHING.md for the new scoped lava-fishing prototype, particularly
    native rod input, bobber/line rendering, biome catches and fish catch items.
